📋 Analysis Framework

🪜 The Five-Rung Ladder

Each rung adds exactly one thing to the rung below.

  • Metric — a number
  • Comparative Metric — …vs a reference
  • Signal — …watched over time
  • Finding — …composed with other facts
  • Insight — …who it matters to & why

Tiers 1–3 live in region_metrics (one source of truth). Tiers 4–5 are generated on demand.

📊 Tier 1 · Metric

A single value at one point in time. A Sum or level — no comparison yet.

  • e.g. MAT Sales = €4.2M
  • units, sales_eur, market_share

MAT Sales = -10%? No — it's just €4.2M.

➗ Tier 2 · Comparative Metric

One value measured against one reference. Still a single number — but now it means something.

  • vs last year: growth_py · vs last period: growth_pp
  • vs national avg: mshare_deviation
  • vs forecast: growth_vs_fcst

MAT Sales Growth = MAT Sales now vs last year = −10%.

📈 Tier 3 · Signal

The same comparative metric watched over time — its own trajectory. Nothing more.

"MAT Sales Growth is −10% now — but last month it was −30%, and 6 months ago −40%."

The signal: things aren't as bad as the −10% looks in isolation — they're recovering. The signal definition ends here.

  • Stored shape: rank_trend_3m, growth-of-growth (growth_pp_mshare_dev)

🧩 Tier 4 · Finding

Open-ended composition of any lower facts — metrics, comparative metrics, and (usually several) signals. Rarely one fact; in theory it could be.

"Sales Growth recovering (−40→−30→−10) AND competition vanishing AND now meeting forecast."

Compose however you like — or not: IF/THEN rules, clustering, classification, LLM, or just a bundle of signals. Archetypes: Star · Turnaround · Slowing Giant · Deteriorating · Riding Wave · Weak Foundation · Dangerous Stability.

🎯 Tier 5 · Insight

Finding + business context: to who, when, for what, and how it matters. The same finding produces a different insight — or none at all — per persona.

From the recovering-region finding:

  • Sales Manager (all territories, monthly, plans with rep) → "Rep seems on track to recover an important region."
  • Sales Rep (own territory in detail) → "Turnaround looks possible — keep pushing. Check all accounts for order status."
  • CEO (only the most problematic) → nothing surfaced — region is improving.

🔍 Cross-cutting · Relevance

A filter, not a tier. Decides which signals are worth composing and which findings are worth surfacing.

Strength = Materiality × Magnitude × Persistence × Horizon

  • Materiality + Persistence >> Magnitude
  • A −4% slide in a top-10% region beats a −40% blip in a tiny one

📊 Computed Metrics · Formulas & Definitions

Market Share

market_share = (own_sales / total_franchise_sales) × 100%

Region's brand sales as percentage of total franchise market (own + competitors). Range: typically 0–60% depending on franchise & region size.

Market Share Deviation

mshare_deviation = market_share − national_avg_market_share

Signed deviation from national average. +0.5pp = outperforming; −2.6pp = underperforming. Better lens than absolute market share for regional comparison.

Growth PY Sales

growth_py_sales = ((current_sales − prior_year_sales) / prior_year_sales) × 100%

Year-over-year growth rate on sales. Computed per period type (Month, RollQ, YTD, MAT). Positive = growth; negative = decline.

Growth PP Sales

growth_pp_sales = ((current_sales − prior_period_sales) / prior_period_sales) × 100%

Sequential growth in sales vs prior period. Shift size depends on period type: Month→1, RollQ→3, YTD→12, MAT→1.

Growth PY Units

growth_py_units = ((current_units − prior_year_units) / prior_year_units) × 100%

Year-over-year growth rate on units. Mirrors growth_py_sales but tracks volume instead of value. Useful for volume-driven analysis.

Growth PP Units

growth_pp_units = ((current_units − prior_period_units) / prior_period_units) × 100%

Sequential growth in units vs prior period. Tracks period-on-period volume changes independent of pricing.

Growth PY Market Share Deviation

growth_py_mshare_dev = mshare_deviation[current] − mshare_deviation[prior_year]

Year-over-year change in market share deviation. Positive = improving relative position; negative = losing relative ground. Measured in percentage points.

Growth PP Market Share Deviation

growth_pp_mshare_dev = mshare_deviation[current] − mshare_deviation[prior_period]

Period-over-period change in market share deviation. Isolates recent momentum in relative market position. Measured in percentage points.

Growth vs Forecast EUR

growth_vs_fcst_eur = growth_py_sales − national_brand_fcst_growth_py

Actual YoY sales growth minus national forecast growth (pp). Positive = beat forecast. For declining products, −5% actual vs −10% forecast = +5pp advantage.

Growth vs Forecast Units

growth_vs_fcst_units = growth_py_units − national_brand_fcst_growth_py_units

Actual YoY units growth minus national forecast units growth (pp). Parallel to growth_vs_fcst_eur but measures volume vs value performance.

Rank Units

rank_units = NTILE(100) of units within (year_month, period_type, brand)

Percentile rank 1–100 of unit volume per period. 100 = highest unit volume region. Complements rank_sales_eur for volume-based analysis.

Rank Sales EUR

rank_sales_eur = NTILE(100) of sales_eur within (year_month, period_type, brand)

Percentile rank 1–100 per period. 100 = highest-value region for that brand. Recomputed per period_type — rank can shift between Month, RollQ, MAT views.

Rank Market Share Deviation

rank_mshare_dev = NTILE(100) of mshare_deviation within (year_month, period_type, brand)

Percentile rank of market share deviation from national average. 100 = highest relative market share; 1 = lowest relative market share.

Rank Growth PY

rank_growth_py = NTILE(100) of growth_py_sales within (year_month, period_type, brand)

Percentile rank of year-over-year growth. 100 = highest growth region; 1 = lowest / most negative growth.

Rank Growth PP

rank_growth_pp = NTILE(100) of growth_pp_sales within (year_month, period_type, brand)

Percentile rank of period-on-period growth. 100 = best sequential growth; 1 = worst sequential growth.

Rank Growth PY Market Share Deviation

rank_growth_py_mshare_dev = NTILE(100) of growth_py_mshare_dev within (year_month, period_type, brand)

Percentile rank of YoY market share deviation change. 100 = strongest improvement in relative position; 1 = greatest loss.

Rank Growth PP Market Share Deviation

rank_growth_pp_mshare_dev = NTILE(100) of growth_pp_mshare_dev within (year_month, period_type, brand)

Percentile rank of period-over-period market share deviation change. Highlights regions with recent positive or negative momentum in relative position.

Rank Growth vs Forecast EUR

rank_growth_vs_fcst_eur = NTILE(100) of growth_vs_fcst_eur within (year_month, period_type, brand)

Percentile rank of sales growth vs forecast delta. 100 = beat forecast most; 1 = most below forecast. For declining products, beating forecast still deserves a high rank.

Rank Growth vs Forecast Units

rank_growth_vs_fcst_units = NTILE(100) of growth_vs_fcst_units within (year_month, period_type, brand)

Percentile rank of units growth vs forecast delta. Parallel to rank_growth_vs_fcst_eur but measures volume performance vs forecast.

Rank Trend 3M

rank_trend_3m = rank_sales_eur[t] − rank_sales_eur[t−3]

Change in volume rank over 3 periods. Positive = improving; negative = declining. Isolates recent momentum independent of absolute volume.
